TestTube is an ad-free YouTube client. Home, search, queue and history work without an account; signing in is optional and only used for Subscriptions.

<b>Features</b>
* <b>Ad-free playback</b>
* <b>Suggested videos and a next video that always plays</b>
* <b>Description and Comments tabs under the player</b>
* <b>Mini-player support</b>
* <b>Local queue support</b>
* <b>Background & Picture-in-Picture support</b>
* <b>Built-in video downloader</b>
* <b>Live stream chat support, etc.</b>
